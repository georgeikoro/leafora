Wholesale

For offices, shops and projects

The same plants, the same eighteen months, in larger numbers. The only real difference is that we need to know in advance.

Who this is for

Offices that want more than one plant in reception, cafes and shops planning a fit-out, stylists buying for a job, and anyone else who needs more than one or two at a time.

What does not change

Nothing we sell grows faster because the order is bigger. A plant takes eighteen months regardless of who is buying it, so a wholesale order books a place in the schedule rather than jumping the queue.

How it works

Tell us how many, which species, and the date you need them by. If we have it ready in the greenhouse, we confirm a price and a delivery window. If we do not, we talk about what can actually be ready by then, rather than promising something we cannot keep.

Pricing

There is no public wholesale price list, because it depends on species, size and quantity. Send a question and you get an answer with real numbers, not a form to fill in.

Tell us what you need

Quantity, species, and by when. A grower answers within a working day.
