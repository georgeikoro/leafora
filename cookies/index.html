Cookies

Cookies, and the ones we do not use

This site sets no advertising cookies and shows no consent banner, because there is nothing here that needs consenting to.

Draft copy. This is plain-English placeholder policy written for a design build. Have a solicitor read it before Leafora trades.

What is stored

One entry in your browser called leafora:saved, holding the shortlist of plants you have saved. It is local storage rather than a cookie, it never leaves your device, and clearing site data removes it.

Nothing else. No analytics cookie, no session cookie until you have a basket to keep, no third-party pixel.

Why there is no banner

A banner is required for cookies that are not strictly necessary: tracking, advertising, cross-site measurement. We do not set any, so asking you to click Accept would serve no purpose.

If we ever add analytics, it will be a version that stores nothing on your device, and this page will say so before it goes live.

What our suppliers set

The typefaces are served from this site rather than a font CDN, so reading a page here contacts nobody else. The payment provider will set its own cookies during checkout; those are strictly necessary to take a payment and are covered by their policy.

Removing what is there

Clear site data for leafora.example in your browser settings, or remove everything from your saved list. Both do the same job.
