What to expect

What you can expect from us when you buy a living thing by post, and what we expect back.

Draft copy. This is plain-English placeholder policy written for a design build. Have a solicitor read it before Leafora trades.

Buying

A price on this site includes the pot and VAT. It does not include delivery, which is added at checkout.

An order is accepted when we email to say the plant is packed, not when you pay. If something has been miscounted in the greenhouse we will refund you the same day rather than send a plant we are not happy with.

Delivery

We dispatch Tuesdays and Thursdays and aim to be with you inside three working days. We do not ship when the forecast is below two degrees; your order waits and we tell you it is waiting.

Someone needs to be present to take the box in. A plant left behind a bin in July is not a plant we can replace under the guarantee.

The thirty-day guarantee

If a plant dies within thirty days, send us a photograph and we replace it. No return postage, no argument. It applies once per plant, and it does not cover a plant that has clearly been repotted, divided, or left outside in frost.

Support continues after thirty days. It stops being a replacement and becomes advice, which is usually all that is needed.

Returns

Live plants are excluded from the usual fourteen-day change-of-mind right, because a plant that has spent a week in a hallway and gone back in a box is no longer sellable. Pots, tools and gift cards can be returned unused within fourteen days.

Things we cannot promise

Plants are individuals. Leaf count, height and the exact pattern of splits vary within the size you ordered. The photographs on this site show the size stated and are never of a larger plant.

Last updated 3 September 2026. If we change anything that affects you, the change is emailed to anyone on the nudge list before it takes effect.
