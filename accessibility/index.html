Accessibility

Where it stands, and where it does not

An honest list rather than a promise of perfection, and an address to write to when something does not work.

What is in place

The site is built with plain, semantic HTML, can be navigated entirely by keyboard, and respects a system setting for reduced motion: the moving parts in the hero and the film section stay still if you have asked your device for that. The diagnosis tool and the size options on product pages are real form controls, not just clickable boxes.

Every photograph of a plant or a room has alt text describing what is actually shown. Text-to-background colour contrast has been checked against common guidelines on both the light and dark backgrounds.

What we know is still missing

The spinning ring of icons in the hero is purely decorative and hidden from screen readers, but it has not been tested against every screen reader. A handful of older pages may be missing a visible focus ring on some single element. If you find a spot like that, we would like to know.

If something does not work

Tell us which page, what you were trying to do, and what device or assistive technology you were using. Send it through the contact page, and we treat it as a real bug to fix, not a footnote.

Report a problem

As specific as you can be, ideally with the page's address. We answer within a working day.
