Our growers

The people at the other end

When you send a photograph, somebody who has grown that exact species reads it. Here is what that means in practice.

One species, one grower

Every plant we sell has somebody who is responsible for it from cutting to dispatch. They know how that species behaves in a Kent greenhouse in February, which often differs from generic advice found online.

That is why the answer you get to a question is specific. It is not a support agent reading a page.

They answer their own messages

Messages are not routed through a help desk. The grower for that species reads the photograph, and answers within a working day. If the answer is "you are doing nothing wrong, that is a normal leaf drop", you get that answer too.

What they will not do

They will not tell you to buy a replacement when the plant can be saved, and they will not recommend a feed, a tonic or a gadget to fix a light problem. Light problems are fixed by light.

Visiting

The Hackney room is open Tuesday to Saturday and somebody is usually potting something. You are welcome to visit, ask what is on the bench, and take a cutting home if we have spares. The hours and what to expect are on the visit us page.

For anything else

A photograph of the plant and one of the room is usually all a grower needs.
