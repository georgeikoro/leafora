Ask a grower

Someone who has actually grown the plant reads every message, and answers within a working day. There is no wrong question and no charge, whether you bought the plant here or inherited it from a flatmate.

If a plant is unwell, say what changed recently: a move, a repot, the heating going on.

We reply from a real address, not a no-reply one.

Send a photograph

Nine times out of ten a picture settles it faster than a paragraph. Three shots tell us almost everything:

The whole plant, pot included, so we can see its proportions. The problem close up, including the underside of the leaf if there is anything on it. And the room, taken from where the plant stands, facing the window.

Attach them to your reply once we answer, or bring them when you visit.
