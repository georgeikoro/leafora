Pet safe

None of these are pet safe

This page could have shown a filtered list of every plant we grow. None of them would have been accurate.

The honest answer

Monstera and Ficus lyrata both contain calcium oxalate crystals. A cat or dog that chews a leaf gets a burning mouth, drooling, and sometimes vomiting. It is rarely fatal and it is genuinely unpleasant.

Everything in our greenhouse today is one of those two species, so nothing we sell belongs on a pet safe list.

What we recommend instead

Height works. A monstera on a plant stand or a shelf is out of reach of most dogs and uninteresting to most cats, who are drawn to dangling leaves rather than the plant itself.

A cutting in a glass on a bookshelf is the safest thing we sell, for the simple reason that nothing can reach it.

If you have a determined chewer, buy from someone who grows calathea, marantas, parlour palms or spider plants. We can recommend a supplier.

When that will change

Calathea and parlour palm are in trial in the greenhouse now. When they are ready, this page will list them instead.

For anything else

A photograph of the plant and one of the room is usually all a grower needs.
