Delivery and returns

Posting a living thing

Dispatch days, the cold-weather hold, and what the thirty-day guarantee covers.

When it leaves

We dispatch on Tuesdays and Thursdays. An order placed on Friday goes out on Tuesday, which is deliberate: a plant that sits in a depot over a weekend arrives worse than one that waits two days in a greenhouse.

Delivery is £6.50, and free over £120. Everything travels with a tracked 24 hour service.

The cold hold

Below 2°C we do not ship. A tropical plant in an unheated van overnight looks fine on arrival and turns black four days later, in your house.

When that happens your order waits and we email you to say so. You may ask us to send it regardless. We will, but the guarantee will not cover a plant sent at your request in cold conditions.

Receiving the delivery

A box left in the sun on a doorstep in July, or on a step in February, is a different plant by the time you open it. Leave a safe place with the courier if you will be out, and open the box the day it lands.

The thirty-day guarantee

If a plant dies within thirty days, send a photograph and we replace it. No return postage, no argument, no need to send the dead one back.

It applies once per plant. It does not cover a plant that has been repotted, divided, left outside in frost, or asked for against our cold-weather advice.

Support continues after thirty days. It stops being a replacement and becomes advice, which is usually all that is needed.

Pots, tools and gift cards

Anything that is not alive can come back unused within fourteen days for a full refund. Live plants are excluded from that right, because a plant that has spent a week in a hallway and gone back in a box is no longer sellable.

For anything else

A photograph of the plant and one of the room is usually all a grower needs. Shorter answers to the most common questions also live on the FAQ page.
