FAQ

Questions that come up often

Shipping, the guarantee, gift cards and visiting, gathered in one place. If the answer is not here, ask a grower directly.

Shipping

Which areas do you deliver to?

The UK only, for now. Every order travels with a tracked 24 hour service, dispatched on Tuesdays and Thursdays.

Do you ship abroad?

Not yet. A flight or a customs delay would undo everything the eighteen months in the greenhouse were for, so we stay on the ground and within the UK until a longer journey is one we trust the plant to survive.

How much does delivery cost?

£6.50, and free over £120. See delivery and returns for the full policy.

What happens if the weather turns cold?

Below 2°C we do not ship. Your order waits in the greenhouse instead, and we email to say so. It is not a popular policy, but it protects the plant.

Our guarantee

What does the thirty days cover?

If a plant dies within thirty days, send a photograph and we replace it. No return postage, no argument, no need to send the dead one back.

What does it not cover?

A plant that has been repotted, divided, left outside in frost, or sent at your request against our cold-weather advice. It applies once per plant.

Can I return pots, tools or gift cards?

Anything that is not alive can come back unused within fourteen days for a full refund. Live plants are excluded from that right, for the same reason as above.

What happens after thirty days?

Support continues. It stops being a replacement and becomes advice, which is usually all that is needed.

Gift cards

Do gift cards expire?

No. No expiry date, no monthly fee, and it can be spent across several orders until the balance runs out.

Does the recipient get something physical?

A code by email on the date you choose, or printed on card stock and posted to you to hand over, at no extra cost. See gift cards.

What if they spend less than the value?

Whatever is left stays on the card. A balance can go on a plant now and a pot in six months.

Visiting

Can I visit?

Yes. The Hackney room is open Tuesday to Saturday. See visit us for the hours and what to expect.

Can I visit the greenhouse in Kent?

Not currently. Kent is a working nursery, not a visitor site. The Hackney room is where you can see and handle a finished plant.

Still have a question

Send it to a grower. We answer within a working day, and there is no wrong question.
