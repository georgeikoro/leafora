About

A greenhouse in Kent.
A room in Hackney.

Leafora is two places. Plants are grown slowly under glass an hour outside London, then moved to a room with ordinary windows and ordinary heating, where they have to prove they can live like that before anyone is allowed to buy them.

How we grow our plants

  1. 01

    Slowly

    A monstera can be pushed to sale size in six months under heat and feed. Ours take about eighteen. The difference is a thicker stem, and leaves that hold their shape in a normal room.

  2. 02

    Without growth regulators

    Large nurseries dose plants to keep them compact on a shelf. It wears off in your house and the plant grows into something you did not choose. We do not use them.

  3. 03

    In peat-free mix

    Bark, coir and pumice, blended for drainage, because the commonest way to kill a houseplant is to leave its roots standing in water. The mix does half that work for you.

  4. 04

    Then acclimatised

    A greenhouse is even light and eighty per cent humidity. A flat is neither. The last month is spent in a room like yours, so the shock happens here rather than there.

What we will not do

We only sell plants we grew ourselves. There is no drop-shipped stock and no third-party warehouse, which is the reason the catalogue stays small rather than growing into a warehouse.

We will not send anything into a cold snap. When that happens, the order waits in the greenhouse and we send an email to say so. It is not a popular policy, but it protects the plant.

Nothing here is dyed, painted, glued or glittered. If a plant needs decorating to be worth having, we would rather grow a better plant.

Every photograph on this site shows the size stated. We have never shot a larger plant than the one that goes in the box, which is a lower standard than it sounds, given what much of the trade sells instead.

Why it matters

A houseplant is a strange thing to sell online. It is alive, heavy, fragile, and it will outlive the transaction by a decade if it is any good. Most of the trade solved that by making plants cheap enough to be disposable.

We would rather grow them well enough to be kept, then stay reachable for as long as you own one. That is the whole business: grow slowly, pack properly, answer the message. The care card, the watering nudge, the grower reading your photograph: different forms of the same idea.

Visit, or send a message

The Hackney room is open Tuesday to Saturday and you are welcome to visit. If that is not practical, a message works just as well.
