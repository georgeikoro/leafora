Visit us

A room in Hackney, open to anyone

You do not need to order anything to come by. Wanting to see a plant before you decide is reason enough.

Hours

Open Tuesday to Saturday, 9 till 5.

This is the room where every plant spends its final month before it ships, not a showroom built for visitors. What you see is what is actually going on.

What you will find there

Plants in their final acclimatising month, usually somebody repotting or packing an order, and generally a pot of coffee. Ask what is on the bench; it is rarely the same thing two weeks running.

If you take something home

If we have spare cuttings that day, you are welcome to one, at no charge. If you want to buy something that is already ready to go, you can usually take it with you on the spot rather than waiting for a dispatch day.

What it is not

There is no greenhouse to see in Hackney; that is an hour away in Kent and is a working nursery, not open for visits. There is also no booked viewing schedule here, just come by within the hours above.

Cannot make it in

A photograph of the room you have in mind works almost as well as a visit. A grower answers within a working day.
