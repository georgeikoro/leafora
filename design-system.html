Design system

The tokens, type, components and voice that hold leafora.example together — pulled directly from the live styles.css / shop.css, not redrawn. Click any swatch to copy its hex.

Foundations

Color

Two grounds carry the whole site: the dark ink hero/footer sections, and the warm paper shop pages. Lime is the one accent — used sparingly, never as a background for large text blocks.

Ink surface Hero, footer, mobile nav drawer. Lime for accents and links, translucent white for hairlines.
Paper surface Every shop page: plants, care guide, journal, checkout. Forest for text and buttons, ink-tinted borders at 16% opacity.
Foundations

Typography

Two families. Cabinet Grotesk carries every heading — bold, tight tracking, never used below 20px. Switzer does everything else: body copy, labels, buttons, form fields.

Every plant comes with a card that describes its needs, and a grower who knows them best. The rest comes down to consistency.

These are the real files, embedded from public/fonts/ — the same Cabinet Grotesk and Switzer woff2s the live site self-hosts, from Fontshare under the ITF Free Font License. That license's free tier appears to expect delivery through Fontshare's own API rather than self-hosted files, which the site currently does — still worth confirming directly with Fontshare, independent of this page.

Spacing & shape

One fluid pair of tokens drives every section's breathing room, and radius scales with what it's wrapping.

--pad-x: clamp(18px, 3.2vw, 60px) --pad-y: clamp(14px, 1.7vw, 26px)
999px — pills, buttons, chips, badges 14px — inputs, cards 16–24px — page-level cards, notes
Components

Buttons

Lime is the primary action, reserved for the one thing you most want clicked. Cream and forest fill in as secondary/inverted variants; outline is the quietest option.

Components

Form controls & chips

Inputs sit on a faint translucent fill until focus; filter chips toggle to a solid forest fill when pressed.

Components

Icon buttons & counts

Voice

Plain, specific, first person. Every claim is a fact about how the plants are actually grown, not a mood.

Use

"A monstera can be pushed to sale size in six months. Ours take about eighteen."

"You do not need experience."

"In practice: finger in to the second knuckle. Dry, water it."

Avoid

"Nobody is born with a green thumb." — stock idiom

Hard-coded counts ("nine plants") that go stale as the catalogue grows

Explaining implementation details the reader doesn't need (e.g. "this list lives in your browser, not a server")

Vanilla HTML / CSS / JS, built with Vite. No component framework, no CSS utility library — every class above is hand-written and lives in styles.css (site-wide tokens, hero) and shop.css (everything on a paper ground).
