Pots & tools

Six things, and no more

Every plant already arrives in a pot suited to it, so nothing here is necessary. These are the six objects we kept buying for ourselves, in the sizes our plants are sold in.

A pot with no drainage hole is a bucket. Everything on this page drains. If you already own a beautiful sealed ceramic, keep the plant in its nursery pot and place that inside it. That is what we do too.

  • Pots

    The Speckled Pot

    £24

    Cream stoneware with the freckle we are known for, and a drainage hole that is actually a hole. Comes with its saucer.

    Three sizes · 12, 16 and 20cm
  • Pots

    The Terracotta

    £18

    Unglazed, so the clay breathes and the soil dries evenly. The right answer for anyone who waters too enthusiastically.

    Three sizes · 13, 17 and 21cm
  • Pots

    The Glass Vessel

    £32

    Hand-blown, wide-bellied and narrow-necked, so a cutting stands up on its own and the roots stay lit from behind.

    One size · 15cm tall
  • Tools

    The Brass Mister

    £34

    A fine, even cone rather than a squirt. Solid brass, so it develops a patina instead of a crack, and it can be dismantled to clear a blocked nozzle.

    250ml · refillable
  • Tools

    The Moisture Stick

    £12

    A plain probe with a needle dial, no battery and nothing to pair. Push it two thirds down and read it. We put one in every box.

    20cm probe
  • Tools

    The Snips

    £26

    Carbon steel, short-bladed, sharp enough to cut a stem cleanly rather than crush it. A crushed cut is where rot starts.

    Carbon steel · 16cm

Choosing a size

Pot up one size at a time, never two. If you tell us what you own and how long you have had it, a grower will tell you whether it needs moving at all. The answer is usually not yet.
