Care guide

The watering nudge

A reminder service for the plants you own, written from the same care card that comes in the box.

What it is

One message a week, in the week a plant of yours needs water. It knows which species you own and how your rooms behave in that month, because those are the only two things that change the answer.

It is not a newsletter. There is no offer in it.

What a message looks like

"Your monstera is due this week. Top inch dry? Water until it runs out of the bottom, then tip the saucer." That is the whole message.

Four times a year you also get a seasonal note: when to start feeding, when to stop, when to repot, and what the heating going on will do to your air.

How to stop it

Reply STOP and it stops, permanently, with no confirmation email asking you to reconsider. You can also turn it off in your account settings.

Turning it off never affects an order, and it never removes your right to ask a grower a question.

What we do with the data

Your email address and which plants you told us you own. Nothing else, no tracking, no sale of the list. It is set out in the privacy page.

For anything else

A photograph of the plant and one of the room is usually all a grower needs.
