Care by plant

Ficus lyrata

The fiddle leaf fig has a reputation for being difficult. It is not difficult, it is stubborn about being moved.

Care card Ficus lyrata Bright · water weekly

What it needs

Light
Bright, and it will take an hour or two of direct sun. Turn it a quarter every week or it leans and stays leaning.
Water
Weekly in summer, fortnightly once the heating is on. It hates sitting wet and it hates going bone dry, which is most of the reputation.
Moving it
It drops leaves after a move and shows it a fortnight later, not the same day. Pick the spot and leave it there.
Dusting
Wipe the leaves every month. They are large, flat and how the plant earns a living; a film of dust measurably costs it light.
Feeding
Fortnightly at half strength through the growing season.
Brown patches
Dark brown spots spreading from the middle of a leaf are usually over-watering. Dry brown edges are usually under-watering or a radiator.
Pets
Not pet safe.
