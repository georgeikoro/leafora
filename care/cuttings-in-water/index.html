Care by plant

Cuttings in water

The easiest thing we sell, and the only one where you can see the roots doing their work.

Care card Cuttings in water Any light · change the water

What it needs

Light
Anything but a dark room. Direct sun will green the water with algae, so a bright shelf away from the glass is better than the windowsill itself.
Water
Keep the roots covered and change it every fortnight. Tap water is fine; let it stand an hour if yours is heavily chlorinated.
Feeding
A drop of liquid feed once a month at a quarter strength, no more. Water has no reserves, so a full dose burns.
Cleaning
If the glass films up, rinse it under the tap with your hand rather than a brush. The roots are more robust than they look.
Moving it to soil
Only when the roots are longer than your hand. Sooner than that and the plant has nothing to hold on with. Water roots and soil roots are different; expect a fortnight of sulking after the change.
How long it can stay
Indefinitely. A cutting in water will live for years, though growth is slow.
Pets
Not pet safe, but a glass on a shelf is out of reach, which is the practical answer.
