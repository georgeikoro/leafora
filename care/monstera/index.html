Care by plant

Monstera deliciosa

The one most people start with, and the one most people over-water. It will show what it needs, given the chance.

Care card Monstera deliciosa Bright, indirect · water when dry

What it needs

Light
Bright and indirect. An east window is ideal. It survives a darker corner but stops splitting, and the gaps between leaves get longer.
Water
When the top inch is dry, roughly every ten days in summer and every three weeks in winter. Water until it runs out of the bottom, then empty the saucer.
Humidity
Ordinary room air is fine. Crisp brown leaf edges mean dry air, usually a radiator underneath it.
Feeding
Fortnightly at half strength, March to September. Nothing in winter.
Splits
Fenestration comes with maturity and light. A young plant makes solid leaves and that is normal. No feed makes it split sooner.
Aerial roots
Leave them, tuck them into the pot, or give it a moss pole. Do not cut them off; they are how it holds itself up.
Pets
Not pet safe. See what we recommend instead.
