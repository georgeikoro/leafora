Privacy

What we know about you

Short version: an email address if you give us one, an order address if you buy something, and nothing else beyond what is necessary.

Draft copy. This is plain-English placeholder policy written for a design build. Have a solicitor read it before Leafora trades.

What we collect

If you buy a plant: your name, delivery address, email and phone number, because a courier cannot deliver to an anonymous doorstep. Card details are handled by the payment provider and never reach us.

If you join the watering nudge: your email address, and which plants you told us you own so the reminders are about the right things.

If you message us: whatever you write, and any photographs you attach.

What we do not collect

We do not run advertising trackers, we do not sell or rent lists, and we do not build a profile of you across other websites.

The saved-plants list on this site is kept in your own browser. It is never sent to us, so we could not look at it if we wanted to.

How long we keep it

Order records for six years, because tax law says so. Nudge subscriptions until you unsubscribe. Message threads for two years, because a plant question is often the same plant a year later.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of everything we hold, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. One email does all three, and we answer within thirty days.

Unsubscribing from the nudge takes one word in a reply. It never removes an order record, because we are not allowed to delete those on request.

Who else sees it

The courier gets a name and address. The payment provider gets the payment. The email platform gets the address the nudge goes to. Nobody else: fonts, images and scripts all come from this site.

Last updated 3 September 2026. If we change anything that affects you, the change is emailed to anyone on the nudge list before it takes effect.
